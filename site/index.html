A few useful answers

Before you begin.

How do I discuss a project?+

Call, email or WhatsApp PropCare using the contact links below. Opening hours: Monday–Saturday, 7am–7pm.

Is HomeOS the company’s live booking system?+

No. HomeOS is a hackathon concept. Technician matching is illustrative; downloading the demo does not book a tradesperson. Contact PropCare directly to arrange work.

How does the demo warranty work?+

Mark a repair completed to start its example warranty countdown. A follow-up remains linked to that repair. Any real warranty terms must be confirmed with the company.
