Check a photo of your repair now

Upload a photo of a leak, crack, damp patch or faulty socket. The check names the likely problem, the trade you need and how urgent it is, and outlines the area on your photo.

Your photo goes to Google Gemini through PropCare's server to be checked. It is not stored. Gas smells, sparks and water near electrics are always treated as emergencies, whatever the AI says.

Everything a home needs between repairs, on your phone

When something breaks

  • Photo check with the problem outlined. Likely causes, the right trade and how urgent it is.
  • Speak instead of typing in English, Hindi or Tamil.
  • Emergency mode. One tap to the gas emergency line or PropCare, with safe steps for gas, CO, electrics and floods.
  • Send to PropCare by WhatsApp or email as a job sheet with your photo, and follow it from Requested to Done.

For landlords

  • Gas safety, EICR, EPC and alarm checks for each rental, marked OK, due soon or overdue.
  • Reminders 30 days before a check is due, and when it is overdue.
  • Home Health Score out of 100, with the jobs that win points back.
  • Maintenance passport PDF with checks, repairs and photos, for tenants, buyers or agents.

For PropCare's team

  • Staff job board behind a PIN, most urgent first.
  • Quotes, visit times and messages that show on the customer's timeline.
  • Home-screen widget with open jobs and the next check due.

From photo to booked repair in three steps

  1. Take a photoAdd a few words, or say them. Choose which property it is.
  2. Get a first lookThe likely problem, the trade and the urgency, with an example price band. Safety rules always win.
  3. Send it to PropCareOne tap sends a job sheet and the photo. Quotes and visit times show on the job's timeline.

Your home's data stays on your phone

There is no account and no cloud copy. Jobs, checks and photos live in the app until you send a job to PropCare yourself. Delete everything from the Account tab at any time.

  • No secret keys in the app. The AI key lives only on the server.
  • Only three permissions: internet, notifications and fingerprint unlock. The camera and microphone open through Android itself.
  • Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • Works offline. Without a connection, an on-device check plus the same safety rules takes over.
